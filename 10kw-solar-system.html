SUBSIDY & SIZING AT 10 KW

Does the subsidy apply to a 10 kW system?

Residential and commercial 10 kW systems can use similar hardware, but the financial treatment is different: residential consumers may qualify for PM Surya Ghar CFA, while commercial consumers do not receive the residential CFA.

Residential connection

Central CFA is capped at ₹78,000

For an eligible residential consumer, PM Surya Ghar central CFA reaches its maximum ₹78,000 at 3 kW and does not increase for a 10 kW system. That puts the residential net after central CFA at about ₹4,32,000 (custom) or ₹4,72,000 (Tata). Eligible consumers under Rajasthan's Mukhyamantri Nishulk Bijli/150-units scheme may receive an additional ₹17,000, reducing the effective net further. The system itself should be sized to sanctioned load, annual consumption, roof area and DISCOM requirements — not artificially to chase subsidy.

Commercial / business connection

Commercial economics are separate

The PM Surya Ghar residential CFA does not apply to a commercial connection. An eligible business that owns and uses the solar asset may be able to claim depreciation under applicable income-tax rules, but the actual tax benefit depends on the entity and tax position. For a commercial 10 kW system, we therefore model savings from electricity offset and net metering first, with tax treatment kept separate.

The maximum central CFA for an eligible residential system is ₹78,000. Rajasthan's additional ₹17,000 is conditional on state-scheme eligibility rather than automatic for every household. We handle the PM Surya Ghar and JVVNL paperwork. Full current eligibility is on the PM Surya Ghar subsidy page.

WHAT A 10 KW SYSTEM GENERATES

Think in units, not in "running" appliances

A 10 kW system isn't a generator sized to your load — its output is a curve that peaks near 10 kW for only a couple of hours at midday and tapers off morning and evening. What you actually get is the units under that curve, and in northeast Rajasthan's strong sun that runs above the national average.

Those units are what offset your bill — not real-time load matching. On an on-grid system the grid is your buffer: when generation exceeds instantaneous consumption, the bidirectional meter records export; imported and eligible exported units are then adjusted under the applicable JVVNL/RERC net-metering and billing rules. For a net-metered commercial consumer, daytime self-consumption is useful but is not the only source of value because exported energy can also be adjusted against eligible imports under the applicable rules. The more important design question is whether annual generation is sensibly matched to consumption so the system is not persistently oversized.

10 KW SOLAR FAQ

Common questions about a 10 kW system in Behror & Neemrana

What is the real price of a 10 kW solar system in Behror?

There are two all-inclusive options, both three-phase. The official Tata Power Solar system is ₹5,50,000 (net from ₹4,55,000 after the residential subsidy). Our custom Premier TOPCon system is ₹5,10,000 (net from ₹4,15,000). Both cover panels, inverter, hot-dip galvanised structure, all wiring and earthing, the JVVNL net-metering application and full PM Surya Ghar paperwork — nothing is billed separately.

Does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy apply to a 10 kW system?

Yes, an eligible residential consumer can install a 10 kW system under the applicable rooftop-solar framework, but the central PM Surya Ghar CFA does not increase beyond ₹78,000 once capacity reaches 3 kW. Rajasthan's additional ₹17,000 support is conditional on state-scheme eligibility. On a commercial connection, residential CFA does not apply; eligible businesses may have depreciation benefits under applicable tax rules, which should be checked for the specific entity.

How many units does a 10 kW system generate?

In the Behror–Neemrana belt a 10 kW system generates about 45–52 units on a typical day, averaging around 1,400 units a month and roughly 17,000 units a year. Those units offset your consumption through net metering — the grid buffers the timing, so what matters is whether your monthly generation matches your monthly usage. Northeast Rajasthan's strong sun keeps generation above the national average.

Is a 10 kW system good for a shop or small business?

Yes, where 10 kW is a sensible match for the establishment's consumption, sanctioned load and roof. With net metering, direct daytime use is helpful but exported energy can also be adjusted against eligible imports under the applicable rules, so the key is avoiding persistent oversizing. Commercial consumers do not receive residential PM Surya Ghar CFA; any depreciation benefit depends on the business's tax position.

Do I need a three-phase connection for 10 kW?

Yes. A 10 kW system runs on a three-phase JVVNL connection, which most large homes, farmhouses and commercial properties already have. If your property is still single-phase, we help you apply for a three-phase upgrade as part of the installation and coordinate it with JVVNL. We confirm your connection type and sanctioned load during the free site visit.

Our custom system uses newer, higher-wattage Premier TOPCon panels at ₹40,000 less, with a longer 10-year inverter warranty, fitting 10 kW into fewer panels, with structure, wiring and BOS executed using the Tata SOP practices followed by our team, and serviced directly by us (Global Green Energy). It is our build, not a Tata product — you choose brand-and-official-service, or tech-and-value.

Can a 10 kW solar system run ACs — and how many?Kya 10 kW se AC chalenge — aur kitne?

Easily, and several of them — but think in units, not simultaneous ACs. A 10 kW system generates about 45–52 units on a sunny day (around 1,400 a month). That’s enough to run several ACs across a large home or a shop through the day, or a few ACs plus the rest of a heavy household load. With net metering the grid buffers the timing: your panels bank units by day and you draw them back whenever the ACs run. So the real question is your monthly unit consumption, not how many ACs switch on together.

How many batteries does a 10 kW solar system need?10 kW system ke liye kitni battery chahiye?

On a normal on-grid 10 kW system, none — net metering lets the grid store your surplus and return it later, which is far cheaper than batteries at this size. You add batteries only for genuine outage backup, and the bank is sized to the load you need to keep running and for how long — not to the 10 kW panel size. At 10 kW that’s a three-phase hybrid inverter with a lithium (LiFePO4) bank matched to your critical load; we design the exact configuration after assessing your backup needs on site.

HinglishNormal on-grid 10 kW system par — koi nahi. Net metering se grid aapki extra unit store karke baad me deta hai, jo is size par battery se kaafi sasta hai. Battery sirf sahi outage backup ke liye, aur bank us load ke hisaab se size hota hai jise chalana hai aur kitni der — 10 kW panel size se nahi. 10 kW par ye ek three-phase hybrid inverter ke saath lithium (LiFePO4) bank hota hai jo aapke zaroori load se match ho; exact configuration hum site par dekh kar design karte hain.

How much does a 10 kW solar system cost, with and without a battery?10 kW system ka kharcha kitna hai — battery ke saath aur bina?

An on-grid 10 kW system (no battery, three-phase) is ₹5,50,000 for the official Tata system or ₹5,10,000 for our custom Premier TOPCon build — net from about ₹4,15,000–₹4,55,000 after the residential subsidy (full breakdown above). Batteries aren’t part of a standard on-grid system: net metering stores your surplus with the grid for free, which at 10 kW saves a large battery cost. If you want outage backup, that battery bank is quoted separately once we size it to your load. Note that on a commercial connection the residential subsidy doesn’t apply.
