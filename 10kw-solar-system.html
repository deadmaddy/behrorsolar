10 kW 16–18 panels ~800 sq ft Large home / small commercial establishment — you are here

As a rule of thumb, allow roughly 80 sq ft of clear, shadow-free roof per kilowatt once you account for walkways and panel spacing. A 10 kW array needs around 800 sq ft — comfortable on most farmhouse roofs, large independent houses and commercial rooftops. Where roof space is tight, the higher-wattage Premier TOPCon panels fit the same 10 kW into fewer panels. The free site visit measures your usable area before we quote.

SUBSIDY & SIZING AT 10 KW

Does the subsidy apply to a 10 kW system?

Residential and commercial 10 kW systems can use similar hardware, but the financial treatment is different: residential consumers may qualify for PM Surya Ghar CFA, while commercial consumers do not receive the residential CFA.

Residential connection

Central CFA is capped at ₹78,000

For an eligible residential consumer, PM Surya Ghar central CFA reaches its maximum ₹78,000 at 3 kW and does not increase for a 10 kW system. That puts the residential net after central CFA at about ₹4,42,000 (custom) or ₹4,72,000 (Tata). The additional ₹17,000 Rajasthan state benefit may apply to eligible beneficiaries under the solar-linked 150-units-per-month Nishulk Bijli scheme. Existing free-electricity enrollment alone does not confirm payment. Your connection’s state-scheme registration and DISCOM eligibility must be checked before this benefit is included in an estimate. State eligibility and disbursement are separate from central CFA redemption. The system itself should be sized to sanctioned load, annual consumption, roof area and DISCOM requirements — not artificially to chase subsidy.

Commercial / business connection

Commercial economics are separate

The PM Surya Ghar residential CFA does not apply to a commercial connection. An eligible business that owns and uses the solar asset may be able to claim depreciation under applicable income-tax rules, but the actual tax benefit depends on the entity and tax position. For a commercial 10 kW system, we therefore model savings from electricity offset and net metering first, with tax treatment kept separate.

For a business connection, start with a commercial solar assessment based on the property and load rather than a fixed residential size.

The maximum central CFA for an eligible residential system is ₹78,000. The additional ₹17,000 Rajasthan state benefit may apply to eligible beneficiaries under the solar-linked 150-units-per-month Nishulk Bijli scheme. Existing free-electricity enrollment alone does not confirm payment. Your connection’s state-scheme registration and DISCOM eligibility must be checked before this benefit is included in an estimate. State eligibility and disbursement are separate from central CFA redemption. We handle the PM Surya Ghar and JVVNL paperwork. Full current eligibility is on the PM Surya Ghar subsidy page.

Common questions about a 10 kW system

My summer AC bill is high. Do I need 10 kW?

Possibly. If your annual consumption is around 12,000 units or more and several ACs run for long hours, we'd assess 10 kW. Show us all 12 bills. A high May or June bill alone isn't enough to decide, especially if your consumption falls sharply during the rest of the year.

What will I pay for 10 kW after Rajasthan subsidies?

Our listed prices are ₹5,20,000 for custom Premier and ₹5,50,000 for Tata Power Solaroof. With approved and received ₹95,000 combined residential support—₹78,000 central plus ₹17,000 Rajasthan—the effective cost is ₹4,25,000 or ₹4,55,000. With central support only, it is ₹4,42,000 or ₹4,72,000. Installation, subsidy filing, JVVNL net metering and GST are included; any custom fabrication is quoted upfront.

How many panels and how much roof space does 10 kW need?

Our Tata option uses seventeen 595 Wp panels, totalling 10.115 kWp. The custom Premier option uses sixteen 620 Wp panels, totalling 9.92 kWp. Allow roughly 800–1,100 sq ft, depending on panel spacing and roof conditions. Tanks, parapets and cleaning access can reduce the usable area.

Can one 10 kW system reduce bills for two meters?

Possibly, through an approved group or virtual net-metering arrangement. It doesn't happen automatically because both meters belong to your family. Group net metering requires eligible connections in the same consumer's name and DISCOM area. We need to check both accounts, the connection categories and JVVNL's process before including that saving.

Ask us to assess both connections

Can I get the ₹95,000 residential support for my shop?

No, this residential support doesn't apply if the connection is commercial. The potential ₹95,000 total is ₹78,000 central plus ₹17,000 Rajasthan state benefit for eligible residential beneficiaries. A shop and a home can use the same size system but have different billing and subsidy treatment. We calculate a shop's savings without assuming these residential benefits.

Read about commercial solar

Is 10 kW worthwhile if my consumption is high only in summer?

Sometimes, but we'd compare it with a smaller system first. If winter consumption is low, much of the extra generation may become surplus, with a different value from electricity you use yourself. The extra cost needs to make sense over the whole year, not just during peak summer.

Can a 10 kW system make my electricity bill zero?

It can, but the size alone doesn't guarantee it. Your annual consumption, generation, connection category and applicable bill credits all matter. Our three zero-payable JVVNL bills are from a separate 3 kW residential installation. They show one account's result, not a promised outcome for a 10 kW home or commercial connection.

Will my 10 kW solar work during a power cut?

An on-grid system shuts down during power cuts for safety reasons, regardless of its size. For backup, we recommend a hybrid system with a battery bank. Running lights and fans is a different requirement from running several ACs. We size the backup around the appliances and hours you need, and quote it separately.

How long does a 10 kW installation take, and when does the subsidy arrive?

The complete process usually takes 3–4 weeks, including installation and JVVNL net metering. We check your existing three-phase connection and any required changes before confirming the schedule. For an eligible residential installation, our usual subsidy estimate is another 2–3 weeks after commissioning. Central and eligible ₹17,000 Rajasthan state credits are processed separately; approvals or connection work can affect the dates.
