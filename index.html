How the subsidy works

Under PM Surya Ghar, eligible residential consumers receive central CFA of ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for the additional capacity between 2 and 3 kW. The central CFA is capped at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above.

Rajasthan consumers who separately qualify under the applicable 100/150-units-free state scheme may receive an additional ₹17,000 through RRECL, taking total government support to up to ₹95,000 for an eligible household.

We confirm eligibility before including the Rajasthan benefit in a customer's financial estimate.

Can a solar electricity bill reach ₹0?

Yes, a ₹0 payable electricity bill is possible for some households, but it is not guaranteed. The final bill depends on solar generation, household consumption, imported and exported units, JVVNL billing and any government-scheme benefits or adjustments applicable to that consumer.

Consumers enrolled in the 100/150-units-free scheme may have those benefits reflected in their bill. For some consumers, cash compensation and adjustments against fixed charges already paid can also affect the final payable amount.

PM SURYA GHAR & NET METERING

Common questions about solar subsidies in Rajasthan

What is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and who qualifies in Rajasthan?

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is a central government scheme that provides a direct subsidy on rooftop solar installations for residential households. In Rajasthan, eligible applicants are residential electricity consumers with a valid JVVNL connection. The system must be installed by a registered vendor and connected to the grid via net metering. Commercial and industrial connections are not eligible under this specific scheme.

How much subsidy can an eligible household in Rajasthan receive?

Eligible residential consumers receive central CFA of ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for the additional capacity between 2 and 3 kW, capped at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above. Eligible Rajasthan consumers enrolled in the 100/150-units-free Mukhya Mantri Nishulk Bijli Yojana may separately receive a ₹17,000 RRECL state top-up, taking total support up to ₹95,000. We confirm eligibility before including the state benefit in your estimate. Global Green Energy is the vendor name used for the PM Surya Ghar process.

Does PM Surya Ghar apply to rural households in Behror Rural and Neemrana Rural?

Yes — the scheme applies to both urban and rural residential consumers anywhere in Rajasthan, provided the household has an existing metered electricity connection from the state discom. Rural households in areas such as Behror Rural and Neemrana Rural served by JVVNL are eligible. If your property is on an agricultural or commercial connection rather than a domestic tariff, a separate assessment is needed before applying.

What is net metering and how does the approval process work in Rajasthan?

Net metering lets your rooftop solar system export surplus electricity to the grid instead of wasting it. The discom installs a bidirectional (net) meter that records both what you import from the grid and what you export to it, and at billing time the exported units are set off against the imported units — so you are billed only for your net consumption, and in effect the meter runs backwards while you generate. In Rajasthan the connection falls under JVVNL: the net-metering application is filed after installation, followed by a technical inspection before the net meter is commissioned. Approval timelines vary with JVVNL's workload but are typically a few weeks. Behror Solar handles the entire process for you — the application, drawings, inspection coordination and follow-up with JVVNL — until your meter is active and exporting, so you never chase the department yourself. Fixed charges and any consumption beyond your generation still appear on the bill.

How long does a full installation take — from site survey to switch-on?

For a standard residential system in Behror or Neemrana, physical installation typically takes one to two days once the structure and materials are on site. The end-to-end timeline from initial site survey to net meter activation is usually three to five weeks, with most of that time spent on discom paperwork and inspection scheduling — not the installation itself. We keep you updated at each stage and manage the discom coordination directly so you do not have to follow up yourself.
