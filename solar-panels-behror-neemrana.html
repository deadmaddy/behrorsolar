DCR vs NON-DCR — WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOU

The panel you choose determines whether you get the subsidy

This is the most important decision a homeowner in Behror or Neemrana needs to make before buying solar.

DCR — Domestic Content Requirement

Made in India — eligible for PM Surya Ghar central CFA when scheme conditions are met

DCR-compliant solar modules are required when claiming central CFA under PM Surya Ghar. For an eligible residential installation, central CFA can be up to ₹78,000. Rajasthan's additional ₹17,000 support is a separate benefit for qualifying consumers under the applicable state scheme and should not be treated as automatic for every household.

  • Required for PM Surya Ghar central CFA eligibility
  • Central CFA up to ₹78,000 for eligible residential systems
  • Additional ₹17,000 Rajasthan support only where the consumer separately qualifies
  • Exact module model and applicable DCR documentation should be confirmed before installation
  • We assist with PM Surya Ghar and JVVNL documentation for eligible projects
Non-DCR — Imported or Non-Certified

High Performance — No Subsidy

Non-DCR modules are not covered by the DCR documentation required for a PM Surya Ghar central-CFA project. They can still be suitable for commercial, industrial, off-grid and other projects where residential subsidy eligibility is not required. Efficiency, technology and price depend on the exact model; DCR status by itself does not determine performance.

  • Not eligible for PM Surya Ghar central CFA
  • Suitable where residential subsidy eligibility is not required
  • Suitable for commercial, industrial and off-grid use
  • Compare the exact model, datasheet and warranty
  • Do not assume DCR or non-DCR status alone determines efficiency
PANEL TECHNOLOGY

Mono PERC, TOPCon and HJT — what is the difference?

All three technologies convert sunlight into electricity. The difference is in how efficiently they do it and how they perform in Rajasthan's heat.

Mono PERC, TOPCon and HJT — what is the difference?
Technology Typical Efficiency Heat Performance Low-Light Output Cost Availability
Mono PERC 19% – 23.5% Good Good Lowest Wide
TOPCon 21% – 24% Better Better Moderate Wide
HJT 23% – 25% Best Best Highest Limited

TOPCon modules can offer favourable temperature and low-light characteristics, but the result depends on the exact module and system design. For Behror and Neemrana, compare the actual datasheet, warranty, roof layout and measured system evidence rather than choosing on the technology label alone.

Technology labels alone do not tell the full story. See our TOPCon vs Mono PERC field test for measured output from real systems and the limits of that comparison.

SOLAR PANEL FAQ

Common questions about solar panels in Behror & Neemrana

What is a DCR panel and why does it matter for PM Surya Ghar?

DCR stands for Domestic Content Requirement. For a residential system claiming central CFA under PM Surya Ghar, DCR-compliant solar modules are required. Eligible residential systems can receive central CFA of up to ₹78,000. Rajasthan's additional ₹17,000 support is separate and applies only to qualifying consumers under the applicable state scheme, so it should not be presented as an automatic ₹95,000 benefit for every household. Before installation, confirm the exact module model and its applicable DCR documentation.

What does the Tata TP590LG10B DCR certificate show?

The DCR certificate displayed on this page lists TP590LG10B at 590 Wp and 23.3% efficiency, with TP Solar Limited identified as the manufacturer. We use the certificate as model-specific evidence; we do not claim that this makes it the highest-efficiency DCR panel across the entire market.

Can I use non-DCR panels and still get the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

No, not for central CFA under PM Surya Ghar. A residential system claiming the central subsidy must use compliant DCR modules and meet the other scheme requirements. Non-DCR modules remain useful for commercial, industrial, off-grid and other projects where PM Surya Ghar residential CFA is not being claimed.

What is the difference between Mono PERC and TOPCon solar panels?

Both are monocrystalline silicon technologies, but their cell structures differ. TOPCon can offer advantages in efficiency, temperature behaviour and degradation depending on the exact module. For a real buying decision, compare the actual model, documentation, warranty and system design rather than assuming one technology label always performs better. We also publish a real-world TOPCon vs Mono PERC field test so buyers can see measured results together with the limitations of that comparison.
