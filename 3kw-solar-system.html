IS A 3 KW SYSTEM RIGHT FOR YOU?

The most subsidised size — but only if it matches your bill

3 kW is where the PM Surya Ghar subsidy is proportionally largest, which is why it is the most common residential choice. Here is how to know it is your size.

3 kW is a good fit if

Your monthly bill runs ₹2,000–₹3,500

  • You consume roughly 300–450 units a month
  • You run fans, lights, fridge, TV and an AC now and then
  • You have around 250 sq ft of shadow-free roof
  • You want the maximum proportional PM Surya Ghar subsidy
  • You have a JVVNL single-phase connection
Consider a different size if

Your usage is higher

  • Bill ₹4,000–₹7,000 with one or two ACs → a 5 kW system fits better
  • Bill above ₹8,000, or a small shop → look at 10 kW
  • Frequent long power cuts and you need backup → add a battery with a hybrid system
  • Not sure? We size it from your last 12 months of bills, free.

HOW THE SUBSIDY WORKS AT 3 KW

3 kW reaches the maximum central PM Surya Ghar CFA

Under PM Surya Ghar, the central CFA is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the additional 1 kW, taking the maximum central CFA to ₹78,000 at 3 kW. Systems above 3 kW can still be installed, but the central CFA does not increase beyond ₹78,000.

Those units are what offset your bill — solar isn't a generator that powers appliances in real time. On an on-grid system, the bidirectional meter records import and export; eligible exported units are adjusted against imported units under the applicable JVVNL/RERC net-metering and billing rules. So you run whatever you like whenever you like — what matters is that your ~430 units a month of generation match your consumption, taking the bill to near zero.

3 KW SOLAR FAQ

Common questions about a 3 kW system in Behror & Neemrana

What is the real price of a 3 kW solar system in Behror?

There are two all-inclusive options. The official Tata Power Solar system is ₹2,05,000 (net from ₹1,10,000 after subsidy). Our custom Premier TOPCon system is ₹1,85,000 (net from ₹90,000). Both prices cover panels, inverter, hot-dip galvanised structure, all wiring and earthing, the JVVNL net-metering application and full PM Surya Ghar paperwork — nothing is billed separately.

What's the difference between the Tata system and your custom TOPCon system?

Both are premium and both keep your full subsidy — the difference is the accountability model. The Tata system is 100% official: Tata Mono PERC panels, installed to Tata's own SOP, with Tata's own 5-year O&M and product warranties — one national brand behind every component. Our custom system uses newer, higher-wattage Premier TOPCon panels at ₹20,000 less, with a longer 10-year inverter warranty, with structure, wiring and BOS executed using the Tata SOP practices followed by our team, and serviced directly by our own local team (Global Green Energy) with a 5-year O&M. We're upfront that the custom system is our build, not a Tata product — you choose brand-and-official-service, or tech-and-value.

Does the panel really matter, or is a cheaper quote fine?

It matters a lot. The risk at 3 kW isn't Tata versus TOPCon — both are premium — it's a rock-bottom quote built on cheap, low-wattage panels with thin structure and no real service. In our own side-by-side field test, a premium 610W TOPCon panel generated about 17% more per kilowatt than a 530W budget panel on the same roof. Over a 25-year life that gap, plus weaker structures that fail sooner, makes the cheap quote far more expensive. Both our options are premium, DCR-certified panels on a hot-dip galvanised structure with a real 5-year O&M.

How many units does a 3 kW system generate per day?

In the Behror–Neemrana belt a 3 kW system generates about 13–16 units on a typical day, averaging around 430 units a month and roughly 5,200 units a year. As a real local example, a 3 kW system in Nainsukh generated 377 units in under 22 days in May. Clear spring days push it higher; heavy monsoon cloud pulls it down. Northeast Rajasthan's strong sun keeps generation above the national average for the same size.

Why is 3 kW the most popular size for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

The central subsidy is ₹18,000 per kW up to 3 kW, reaching the full ₹78,000 at exactly 3 kW; above that the central amount is capped. So 3 kW is where the subsidy covers the largest proportion of the system cost. Eligible consumers under Rajasthan’s Mukhyamantri Nishulk Bijli/150-units scheme may receive an additional ₹17,000, which can reduce the custom system’s effective net cost to about ₹90,000; this state support is not automatic for every residential consumer.

How long does installation take, and when is my meter switched on?

Typically the installation is completed in about 14 days and the system is switched on with the net meter set in about 21 days from sign-off. In the worst case, installation is done by day 21 and the system switched on by day 28. We handle the JVVNL net-metering application and PM Surya Ghar filing ourselves and confirm approval to you — you don't chase any department.

How long until a 3 kW system pays for itself?

For a household that was paying ₹2,000–₹3,500 a month, the net cost — around ₹90,000 on the custom system or ₹1,10,000 on the Tata system — typically pays back in about 2.5 to 3 years through bill savings. After that the system keeps generating for its full 25-year-plus panel life, so the remaining two decades are effectively free electricity. Exact payback depends on your tariff slab and how much generation you use directly versus export.
