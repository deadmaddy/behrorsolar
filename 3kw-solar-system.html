HOW THE SUBSIDY WORKS AT 3 KW

3 kW reaches the maximum central PM Surya Ghar CFA

Under PM Surya Ghar, the central CFA is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the additional 1 kW, taking the maximum central CFA to ₹78,000 at 3 kW. Systems above 3 kW can still be installed, but the central CFA does not increase beyond ₹78,000.

Can a 3 kW solar system run an AC — even a 1.5 ton AC?Kya 3 kW solar se AC — 1.5 ton bhi — chal sakta hai?

Yes, comfortably. A 3 kW system generates about 15–18 units on a sunny day — easily enough to run a normal 1.5-ton AC for 10+ hours, or an inverter 1.5-ton AC for up to 16 hours. On a hybrid system the panels can run the AC directly for about 6–8 hours during the day. One honest point: even if your system has a battery, we don’t recommend running an AC off the battery during a power cut or at night — an AC is a heavy load that drains a battery fast. Solar runs your AC beautifully by day, and net metering with the grid covers the evenings.

HinglishHaan, aaram se. 3 kW system dhoop wale din lagbhag 15–18 unit banata hai — normal 1.5 ton AC 10+ ghante aur inverter 1.5 ton AC 16 ghante tak chal sakta hai. Din me solar aapka AC aaram se chalata hai, aur shaam ko grid (net metering) sambhal leta hai.

How many ACs can run on a 3 kW solar system?3 kW solar par kitne AC chal sakte hain?

The right way to think about it is units per day, not ACs at once. A 3 kW system makes about 15–18 units a day — enough to run, for example, five ACs for 3 hours each, or one AC for 8–10 hours. It’s the same pool of units. With net metering you don’t have to match usage to the sun: your panels bank units with the grid by day, and you draw them back whenever you run the AC — afternoon or midnight. So your daily unit consumption is what matters, not how many ACs run together.

HinglishSahi tareeka hai roz kitne unit sochna, na ki ek saath kitne AC. 3 kW system din ka lagbhag 15–18 unit banata hai — jaise 5 AC ko 3-3 ghante, ya 1 AC ko 8–10 ghante chala sakte ho. Unit wahi hain. Net metering ke saath usage ko dhoop se match karne ki zaroorat nahi: din me panel grid me unit jama karte hain, aur jab AC chalao tab wapas le lo — dopahar ho ya raat. Toh maayne aapki roz ki unit khapat rakhti hai, na ki ek saath kitne AC.

For most homes, yes. If your usage runs consistently higher — a heavy AC load or a large joint family — a 5 kW system is the better fit, and we’ll size it after a quick look at your bills.

HinglishZyada-tar gharon ke liye, haan. Agar usage isse zyada rehti hai — heavy AC ya bada joint family — toh 5 kW behtar rehta hai, jise aapke bill dekh kar size kar denge.

How many batteries does a 3 kW solar system need?3 kW solar system ke liye kitni battery chahiye?

First, the honest answer: an on-grid 3 kW system needs no batteries — with net metering, the grid is your battery. You add batteries only if you want power-cut backup. If you do, the count depends on your inverter’s battery voltage. For a common 24 V inverter, that’s either two lead-acid batteries, or — what we recommend — a single 25.6 V LiFePO4 (lithium) battery of about 2.5 kWh. LiFePO4 lasts far longer and is maintenance-free, so we steer most homes to it over lead-acid.

HinglishPehle imaandaar jawaab: on-grid 3 kW system me battery ki zaroorat hi nahi — net metering ke saath grid hi aapki battery hai. Battery sirf tab jab power-cut backup chahiye. Agar chahiye, toh number aapke inverter ke battery voltage par depend karta hai. Aam 24 V inverter ke liye ya toh 2 lead-acid battery, ya — jo hum recommend karte hain — ek 25.6 V LiFePO4 (lithium) battery lagbhag 2.5 kWh ki. LiFePO4 bahut zyada chalti hai aur maintenance-free hai, isliye hum zyada-tar ghar ke liye yahi behtar maante hain.

We handle the JVVNL net-metering application and PM Surya Ghar filing ourselves and confirm approval to you — you don't chase any department.

How long until a 3 kW system pays for itself?

For a household that was paying ₹2,000–₹3,500 a month, the net cost — around ₹90,000 on the custom system or ₹1,05,000 on the Tata system — typically pays back in about 2.5 to 3 years through bill savings. After that the system keeps generating for its full 25-year-plus panel life, so the remaining two decades are effectively free electricity. Exact payback depends on your tariff slab and how much generation you use directly versus export.
