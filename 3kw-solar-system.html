Central CFA and Rajasthan support

Eligible residential consumers may receive up to ₹78,000 central financial assistance under PM Surya Ghar, subject to applicable requirements and approval.

Up to an additional ₹17,000 Rajasthan state support may be available to eligible beneficiaries under the solar-linked 150-units-per-month Nishulk Bijli scheme. Existing free-electricity enrollment alone does not confirm eligibility for the solar payment. We check current registration and DISCOM requirements before including it in an estimate. The maximum combined assistance is ₹95,000 only where both benefits apply and are received.

Common questions about a 3 kW system

Is 3 kW enough for my home, or should I choose 5 kW?

If you use under 400 units a month and have no AC or only light AC use, we'd first look at 3 kW. Around 450 units or above, we'd compare it with 5 kW. If another AC is already on your shopping list, tell us now. We check your full year's bills and roof before deciding.

Our 3 kW-class prices are ₹1,85,000 for custom Premier and ₹2,00,000 for Tata Power Solaroof. With approved and received ₹95,000 combined support—₹78,000 central plus ₹17,000 Rajasthan—the effective cost is ₹90,000 or ₹1,05,000. Can a 3 kW system cover my AC consumption?

Yes. If your overall monthly consumption, including the AC, stays around 400–450 units, a 3 kW on-grid system can cover a major part of it. When your appliances need more power than solar is producing, the grid supplies the balance. Coverage changes with the season and shade on your roof.

How many panels and how much roof space does 3 kW need?

Our current Tata option uses six 595 Wp panels, totalling 3.57 kWp. The custom Premier option uses five 620 Wp panels, totalling 3.10 kWp. Plan for roughly 250–325 sq ft of usable, unshaded roof, depending on the layout. We check tanks, parapet shadows and cleaning access before confirming the fit.

Can I start with 3 kW and expand it later?

Yes, if we plan for it now. The inverter, structure and available roof space need to support the extra panels. Otherwise, some equipment may need changing later. Tell us the size you eventually want so we can compare the cost now with the cost of expanding later. JVVNL approval may also need updating.

Can my electricity bill become zero with 3 kW?

Yes, it can—but not every month and not for everyone. We've shared three consecutive JVVNL bills from a 3 kW installation in Behror showing ₹0 payable, even though consumption exceeded solar generation. The remaining charges were covered by the applicable monthly scheme credit. Your result depends on consumption, generation and eligibility.

Will my 3 kW solar work during a power cut?

An on-grid system shuts down during power cuts for safety reasons. If you need electricity during an outage, we recommend a hybrid system with a battery bank. It can use power from the panels when sunlight is available and draw from the battery when needed, within the system's backup capacity.

The complete process usually takes 3–4 weeks, including installation and JVVNL net metering. Our usual subsidy estimate is another 2–3 weeks after commissioning. We handle the central and eligible ₹17,000 Rajasthan state-benefit filing. The credits are processed separately, and approvals or processing delays can affect the dates.

Can a 3 kW system bring a JVVNL bill to ₹0?

A ₹0 payable bill can happen, but is not guaranteed. Solar generation, consumption, scheme credits and other adjustments affect the amount payable. The owner’s bills below each show solar generation and a Mukhya Mantri Nishulk Bijli Yojana adjustment covering the billed total. They do not show that solar alone caused the ₹0 payable amounts.

Annual export settlement: the owner’s experience

In the owner’s JVVNL experience, surplus exported units are settled once a year at approximately ₹3.00–₹3.20 per unit. Accumulated credit can offset a later bill, for example one with ₹300–₹400 in energy charges, and may bring the payable amount to ₹0.

This is separate from the monthly scheme adjustments shown above. The outcome depends on recorded exports, the rate and settlement applied to the account, and other charges and credits. This is one account’s experience, not a guaranteed rate or result.
