Tata Power Solaroof Warranty Guide: Registration, Verification & Claims

If you have bought — or are planning to buy — a Tata Power Solaroof system, there is one document you should know about: the Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited Warranty Certificate.

By Mahendra Yadav · Behror Solar · Updated 11 October 2026

Customers often ask where they have to “register” their Tata solar warranty. The important thing to understand is that there is no public consumer warranty-registration page that the homeowner fills out. The warranty process is handled through the authorised Tata project/channel-partner workflow.

That distinction matters, because buying Tata-branded solar equipment is not by itself the same thing as receiving a Tata Power Solaroof warranty certificate.

This guide explains how the process actually works, what should appear on your certificate, what Tata's current warranty document covers, how to verify it and what to do if you need to make a claim.

IMPORTANT

You do not register a Tata Power Solaroof warranty by filling out a public consumer registration page. The authorised project/channel-partner process is what leads to the system-generated warranty certificate.

The first thing to know: the customer does not register the warranty

For an official Tata Power Solaroof installation, the warranty process is initiated by the authorised channel partner/vendor — not by the end customer.

The process can begin when a Tata kit is ordered against the customer's project or after the installation is marked complete. From the customer's point of view, the important part is that the project is processed correctly and Tata's required installation procedures and SOPs are followed.

There is therefore no need to hunt for a Tata website where you enter your name, phone number and panel serial numbers yourself.

The customer should instead make sure that the company handling the Tata Power Solaroof project is actually capable of completing the authorised process.

Keep your project documents

Even though the customer does not carry out the warranty registration, keep your:

  • quotation or agreement
  • final invoice/payment records
  • DCR certificates where applicable
  • installation and commissioning documents
  • equipment details
  • warranty certificate once issued

These can become important if project details need to be verified later.

What happens behind the scenes

A genuine warranty certificate contains far more information than just the customer's name.

During the Tata Power Solaroof project workflow, the authorised party records information such as the customer and site details, project size, installation information, module serial numbers, inverter serial number, data logger details and supporting commercial/project records.

Equipment traceability is important. Module, inverter and data-logger information may be entered, scanned or photographed so that there is a record connecting the equipment supplied to the final installation. This information can start being captured when the project is ordered and continues through installation.

Tata can also verify whether the installation follows its applicable guidelines and SOPs. Once the required project information is complete, the authorised party submits the project through the relevant Tata process.

The exact internal Tata platform used by authorised parties is not something the customer needs access to in order to receive the warranty.

  1. Tata project/kit linked to customer
  2. Installation completed
  3. Project + equipment serial information recorded
  4. Required information submitted through authorised workflow
  5. Tata verification/processing
  6. Warranty certificate generated
  7. Certificate delivered to customer

What does the Tata Power Solaroof warranty certificate look like?

The actual document is titled:

LIMITED WARRANTY CERTIFICATE

It is issued by Tata Power Renewable Energy Ltd. (TPREL) for the Solar Power Generating System, or SPG System.

It is an online system-generated document with a uniquely generated warranty number and requires no signature.

What information should appear on the certificate?

The certificate we reviewed records:

Certificate fieldWhat it identifies
Project TypeType of Tata solar system
Asset No.Tata project/asset record
Project SizeInstalled project capacity
Purchase Order NumberAssociated project/order
Project LocationInstallation location
Project Owner NameCustomer/project owner
Installation & Commissioning DateRecorded project completion date
Authorized Channel PartnerTata-associated entity responsible for the project
Inverter Serial NumberInstalled inverter
Data Logger Serial NumberMonitoring/data-logger equipment
Module Serial NumbersIndividual solar modules

Those fields are visible on the Tata-issued certificate itself.

This is one reason serial-number capture and proper project documentation matter: the warranty is attached to a defined solar system, not merely to the statement that “Tata panels were installed”.

How long does it take to receive the warranty?

Do not expect the warranty PDF necessarily to arrive on the day the installation is completed.

In our practical experience with the process, the certificate can take from a couple of weeks to a couple of months depending on pending work and processing at channel-partner, state and Tata levels.

That should be treated as a practical observation, not as a guaranteed Tata turnaround time.

Once generated, the certificate may reach the customer through Tata by email. The channel partner may also forward the certificate to the customer; in our workflow, we commonly provide it through WhatsApp as well.

What should you check when you receive it?

Do not simply save the PDF without reading it.

Check at least:

  1. Your name and project details. These should correspond to the actual customer and installation.
  2. Project location. Make sure the installation has been mapped to the correct site.
  3. System capacity. The project size shown on the warranty should correspond to the system installed.
  4. Installation and commissioning date.
  5. Authorized Channel Partner. Check which organisation Tata's own document records against your project.
  6. Inverter and module details. The certificate contains equipment serial information, so this should correspond to the installed project.

Personal/customer information is particularly important, but the complete record should be reviewed.

What if something is wrong?

Contact the channel partner/vendor handling the project.

The authorised party can raise a correction request. Tata can make the correction where the requested change is reasonable and supported by the appropriate documents.

What does the Tata Power Solaroof warranty cover?

This is where a lot of solar-warranty confusion begins.

A phrase such as “30-year solar warranty” does not mean every part of the rooftop solar system is covered for 30 years.

The current Tata certificate we reviewed separates different components:

ComponentWarranty stated in the certificate
Solar PV Modules12-year limited product warranty
Solar PV Modules — output30-year limited power-output warranty
Inverter8 years
Other BOS5 years
Inverter batteriesExcluded from this warranty

The certificate additionally states that the module warranty is subject to the limited warranty certificate of the module OEM.

Product warranty and power-output warranty are not the same thing

The module's 12-year product warranty relates to the module as a product under the applicable warranty terms.

The 30-year power-output warranty relates to the module's warranted output/performance over time and is subject to the OEM's terms.

It should therefore not be described as:

“The whole Tata solar system has a 30-year warranty.”

That is not what the certificate says.

What about the installation itself?

The Tata certificate contains a separate Installation Warranty section.

TPREL states that the system is to be installed in accordance with applicable Indian standards and by experienced designated representatives.

The certificate also contains a strict notification condition for an alleged wrong or defective installation: the buyer is required to notify TPREL within the period specified in the certificate.

The practical rule for the customer is simple:

If you believe something is wrong with the installation, report it immediately rather than waiting.

When does the Tata warranty actually start?

There are two different concepts here:

1. The warranty-processing workflow

This can begin during ordering or installation.

2. The legal Warranty Commencement Date written in the certificate

This is defined by Tata's warranty terms.

The certificate states that warranty commencement is the earlier of:

  • the installation date recorded for the SPG System; or
  • six months after the material was dispatched from TPREL's factory.

For a warranty claim, the customer may need to establish the delivery/installation date using the original invoice or sale receipt.

So the date when someone starts processing your warranty paperwork should not be confused with the contractual Warranty Commencement Date.

How do you verify a Tata Power Solaroof warranty certificate?

This is one of the most useful features of the certificate.

Tata's document itself gives customers a way to authenticate it.

The certificate says customers can contact:

Contact Tata customer service for warranty verification.

Email: care@tatapower.com

to authenticate the document.

The certificate also carries its own unique warranty number.

If you are uncertain whether the warranty PDF you received is genuine, use the contact information provided by Tata rather than relying solely on a screenshot or a message from the installer.

What if you never receive the warranty certificate?

Start with the business or authorised party that sold and contracted the Tata Power Solaroof project to you.

In our experience, a delayed or missing certificate is not necessarily evidence that the system is fake; there can be pending project processing or backlog. But it should not simply be ignored.

Ask the vendor/channel partner to check the status.

The contractual relationship also matters: the company you paid and whose quotation/agreement you accepted should be able to explain what it committed to provide.

Once a warranty has been generated, customers can also seek help retrieving it through the vendor/channel partner and, where necessary, Tata customer support.

“Tata panels” and “Tata Power Solaroof warranty” are not automatically the same thing

This is probably the most important awareness point in this guide.

Tata equipment can move through dealers, distributors, channel partners and installers.

That does not automatically establish that the company putting the equipment on your roof is authorised to complete Tata's Solaroof project process or that it will obtain the Tata system-generated warranty certificate for you.

From our channel-partner experience, there are situations where Tata kits move onward to other vendors but the required Tata procedures are not ultimately completed for the customer.

Before buying, ask the installer five simple questions

  1. Who is the authorised Tata entity responsible for my project?
  2. Will the Tata kit/project be recorded against my customer details?
  3. Will I receive a Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited Warranty Certificate after the installation is processed?
  4. Which company will appear as the Authorized Channel Partner on that certificate?
  5. Who will handle the warranty if there is a problem later?

If the answer is only:

“Don't worry, these are Tata panels.”

ask the remaining questions.

Why can a Tata warranty process get held up?

The warranty certificate is tied to an installation process, not just to purchasing a brand of panels.

Based on our project experience, problems can arise when the party handling the installation does not complete the required process or cuts corners on items such as approved structure, BOS components, grounding or other Tata installation requirements.

Tata may inspect or verify whether applicable guidelines and SOPs have been followed.

That is another reason to establish who is actually responsible for the complete system before choosing an installer.

Tata warranty and PM Surya Ghar documentation are not the same thing

Customers should not treat subsidy paperwork and a Tata warranty certificate as interchangeable documents.

A PM Surya Ghar project can involve warranty and O&M obligations, but how those obligations are provided depends on the actual project/vendor arrangement.

For a Tata Power Solaroof project processed through the authorised Tata route, the Tata warranty certificate is its own project document.

So if you are purchasing a Tata Power Solaroof system, ask specifically about the Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited Warranty Certificate, not only whether your PM Surya Ghar paperwork has been completed.

How to make a Tata Power Solaroof warranty claim

If something goes wrong, report it quickly.

The Tata warranty certificate states that warranty claims must be received within the applicable warranty period and should be made immediately, with a maximum period of two working days from first discovery of the defect under the certificate's stated terms.

A claim should be made in writing and include appropriate information such as:

  • purchase date
  • system/equipment serial information
  • detailed description of the alleged defect
  • invoice
  • supporting project/warranty documents

The certificate says customers may approach their TPREL Channel Partner/Retailer or TPREL customer service for warranty claims and service.

Do not return equipment independently before Tata has provided the appropriate authorisation.

What can Tata do if a warranty claim is accepted?

Under the certificate, TPREL may choose to:

  • repair the defective system/component, or
  • replace it with a new or remanufactured equivalent or better component/system.

Repair or replacement does not restart the entire warranty period. The repaired or replaced component continues for the remainder of the original applicable warranty period.

Things that can affect warranty coverage

The Tata warranty document contains detailed exclusions.

Among them are circumstances involving:

  • misuse, abuse, neglect or accident
  • negligent maintenance
  • failure to follow applicable instructions
  • unauthorised installation, repair or maintenance
  • components not supplied by TPREL as part of the covered system
  • altered or illegible serial numbers/nameplates
  • improper electrical connection
  • certain abnormal environmental conditions
  • specified force-majeure/external events
  • problems in components outside the TPREL-supplied system

This guide is a plain-English explanation. The warranty certificate issued for your project remains the governing document for your particular system.

Can you transfer the Tata Power Solaroof warranty?

The certificate states that the warranty applies to the original purchaser while the SPG System remains physically installed at its original installation location.

It also states that transfer of ownership of the SPG System to a third party makes the warranty void under these terms.

If you are buying or selling a property with an existing Tata Power Solaroof system, this is therefore something worth checking directly with Tata before relying on the remaining warranty period.

What should you save after your Tata solar installation?

Keep a permanent digital folder containing your:

  • Tata Power Solaroof warranty PDF
  • final invoice
  • quotation/agreement
  • DCR/module documentation
  • installation and commissioning records
  • equipment/model details
  • important emails and service correspondence

You may not need them every day, but they become extremely useful when a system is several years old and somebody needs to establish exactly what was supplied.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register my Tata Power Solaroof warranty myself?

No public consumer registration process is required in the workflow described here. The authorised channel partner/vendor handles the project and warranty process.

Should I receive a physical signed warranty certificate?

The Tata certificate we reviewed states that it is an online system-generated warranty document with a unique warranty number and requires no signature.

How long is the Tata solar-panel warranty?

The current certificate reviewed for this guide states 12 years limited product warranty and 30 years limited power-output warranty for Solar PV modules, subject to the module OEM's limited warranty terms.

Does that mean the complete system has a 30-year warranty?

No. The certificate gives different warranty periods to different components. The 30-year term is specifically a limited module power-output warranty.

What is the Tata inverter warranty?

The certificate reviewed for this guide states 8 years for the inverter, excluding batteries.

Is a battery included in this Tata warranty?

The certificate specifically excludes inverter batteries from its scope.

How do I know my Tata warranty certificate is genuine?

Contact Tata customer service for warranty verification. The certificate provides care@tatapower.com.

What if the details on my certificate are wrong?

Contact your channel partner/vendor. The authorised party can raise a correction request, and Tata can make supported corrections.

Who should I contact first if something fails?

The Tata certificate itself says the customer may contact the TPREL Channel Partner/Retailer or Tata customer service.

Does installing Tata panels automatically give me a Tata Power Solaroof warranty?

Do not assume so. Establish which authorised entity is responsible for the project and whether the Tata project/warranty process will actually be completed.
