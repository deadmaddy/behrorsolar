LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Behror Solar respects your privacy and only collects information needed to respond to enquiries, provide solar-related services and support customers.

Information we may collect

When you contact us through our website, phone, WhatsApp or other communication channels, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, general location, property type and any information you voluntarily provide in your message.

If you proceed with a solar project, additional information may be collected where required for quotation, installation, net metering, government-scheme processing, equipment registration or ongoing customer support.

How we use your information

We use enquiry information to:

  • respond to your solar enquiry;
  • understand your requirements and arrange consultation or site assessment;
  • prepare quotations and coordinate installation where applicable;
  • assist with relevant DISCOM, PM Surya Ghar or other project documentation where applicable;
  • provide customer support after installation.

Website enquiry processing

Enquiry forms on BehrorSolar.in are processed through Web3Forms. Submitted enquiries are delivered to our business email account. Our business email service is provided through Spacemail, and enquiry emails are accessed through Gmail.

We currently do not use a CRM for website enquiries.

If an enquiry becomes a customer project, relevant customer and project information may also be recorded in our internal Excel records for ongoing project and customer management.

Retention

Enquiries that do not convert into a customer project are normally deleted within 90 days.

Customer and project records are retained for a longer period because they may remain relevant to the ongoing customer relationship and project history. We do not currently apply a single fixed deletion period to active or past customer records.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to the enquiry or complete a solar project. Depending on the situation, this may include:

  • Web3Forms for website enquiry processing;
  • Spacemail and Google/Gmail for business email handling;
  • the applicable DISCOM or government portals where required for a solar application or project;
  • equipment or installation partners where necessary for project execution, documentation or warranty registration.

Information is only shared to the extent reasonably required for the relevant purpose.

WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication takes place through WhatsApp/Meta and is also subject to their privacy practices.

Google services

BehrorSolar.in may use Google services such as Google Maps for location information and Google Fonts for website typography. These services may process technical information such as your IP address or browser information when they are loaded.

Website hosting and security

Our website uses third-party infrastructure and security services to deliver and protect the website. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information we handle. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customer photos and project information

We may publish genuine solar installation photographs or general project-location information where appropriate. We do not intend to publicly disclose unnecessary private customer information or precise residential location data without an appropriate basis for doing so.

Your information

You may contact us if you want to ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion where appropriate.
