SINGLE-PHASE OR THREE-PHASE?

The one 5 kW decision that depends on your meter

5 kW sits right at the line between the two connection types. Which you need depends on the JVVNL connection your home already has — and we confirm it on the site visit, so there is no guesswork on your side.

Single-phase 5 kW

Tata ₹3,00,000 · Premier ₹2,80,000

Fine for most homes already on a single-phase meter with a normal household load. This is the standard 5 kW system and the price most people are quoting when they say "5 kW".

Three-phase 5 kW

Additional cost*

Needed if your home is already on a three-phase meter, or your sanctioned load and heavy appliances call for it. Larger homes and anyone planning to grow their load later usually go three-phase.

*Shown 5 kW prices are for the standard single-phase configuration. Three-phase is an additional cost for both Tata and Premier Energies, confirmed in your quote.

If you are unsure which meter you have, that is completely normal — send us a photo of your last bill or your meter and we will tell you, and confirm it in person before quoting.

As a rule of thumb, allow roughly 80 sq ft of clear, shadow-free roof per kilowatt once you account for walkways and panel spacing. Around 400 sq ft comfortably takes a 5 kW array. Odd roof shapes, water tanks and parapet shadows all matter — which is exactly what the free site visit checks before we quote. Where roof space is tight, the higher-wattage Premier TOPCon panels fit the same 5 kW into one fewer panel.

Common questions about a 5 kW system

When is 5 kW worth choosing over 3 kW?

If your consumption is around 450 units a month or higher, or you plan to add another AC, 5 kW is worth comparing with 3 kW. The extra generation needs to justify the extra cost. Our listed single-phase prices are ₹2,80,000 for custom Premier and ₹3,00,000 for Tata. With approved ₹95,000 combined support received, the effective cost is ₹1,85,000 or ₹2,05,000.

Compare the full 5 kW prices

Does 5 kW get more subsidy than 3 kW?

No. Eligible Rajasthan households can receive up to ₹95,000: ₹78,000 central plus ₹17,000 state support where both benefits apply. Choosing 5 kW doesn't increase that maximum. If only the central subsidy applies, our listed single-phase packages cost ₹2,02,000 for custom Premier or ₹2,22,000 for Tata after receipt. Choose 5 kW for the extra generation.

Check Rajasthan subsidy eligibility

How many units will a 5 kW system generate?

Around 20–25 units on a clear sunny day is a rough planning figure for a 5 kW panel array. Monthly output changes with the season, clouds, dust, shade and daytime power cuts. Our two packages have slightly different actual panel capacities. We estimate generation for your proposed design and roof rather than promising the same output every day.

Read the generation explanation

Can 5 kW cover two ACs used mainly at night?

It can cover a major part of that consumption, depending on the ACs and how long you run them. With on-grid solar, you use grid electricity at night and daytime exports are accounted for through net metering. You don't need a battery just because you use your AC at night; you need one if you want backup during a power cut.

Do I need a three-phase connection for 5 kW?

Not in every case. We offer single-phase and three-phase options and check your existing connection before recommending one. The listed ₹2,80,000 custom Premier and ₹3,00,000 Tata prices are for single-phase. Any three-phase premium or connection work is explained in your proposal. The inverter must match the approved connection and design.

Compare single-phase and three-phase options

How many panels and how much roof space does 5 kW need?

Our Tata option uses nine 595 Wp panels, totalling 5.355 kWp. The custom Premier option uses eight 620 Wp panels, totalling 4.96 kWp. Allow roughly 400–550 sq ft of usable, unshaded roof, depending on the layout. We confirm the space, shadows and cleaning access during the site visit.

Can my electricity bill become zero with 5 kW?

Yes, it can, if generation and applicable scheme credits cover the bill. It won't happen every month for every household. Will my 5 kW solar work during a power cut?

An on-grid system shuts down during a power cut, even when the sun is shining. If you need backup, we recommend a suitable hybrid system with a battery bank. Tell us which appliances you want to keep running and for how long. That decides the backup capacity and additional cost.

How long does installation take, and when will I receive the subsidy?

The complete process usually takes 3–4 weeks, including installation and JVVNL net metering. Our usual subsidy estimate is another 2–3 weeks after commissioning. We handle the central and eligible ₹17,000 Rajasthan state-benefit filing. The credits are processed separately, and approvals or processing delays can affect the dates.
