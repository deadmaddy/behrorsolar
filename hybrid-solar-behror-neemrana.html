02 · IS HYBRID SOLAR RIGHT FOR YOUR HOME OR BUSINESS?

Solar that does more than reduce electricity bills

A conventional on-grid solar system is an effective way to reduce grid electricity consumption. However, when the grid supply fails, a standard grid-tied inverter shuts down for safety—even when the sun is shining.

A properly designed hybrid solar system adds battery storage and backup capability.

This makes hybrid solar worth considering when:

Your area experiences regular power cuts

Whether outages last a few minutes or several hours, the system can keep selected essential circuits powered within the inverter and battery's operating limits.

You already spend money on electricity backup

If your property relies on a conventional inverter and battery, a hybrid solar system may integrate solar generation and backup management into a more coordinated arrangement.

You need electricity beyond daylight hours

Stored energy can support selected loads during nighttime outages or other periods when solar generation is unavailable.

You operate a shop, office or small commercial property

A suitably designed backup system can support selected essential equipment, lighting, connectivity or other critical loads.

You want a system designed for changing requirements

Battery capacity, inverter capability and circuit planning can be selected according to the property and equipment compatibility.

When is ordinary on-grid solar enough?

If your electricity supply is reliable, your main objective is reducing electricity bills, and you do not need solar-powered backup, an on-grid system may be the simpler and more economical option.

Hybrid solar is not automatically the best choice for everyone. The right solution depends on your electricity consumption, outage pattern, essential loads and available budget.

03 · HOW DOES A HYBRID SOLAR SYSTEM WORK?

One system, three operating situations

A hybrid solar installation coordinates electricity from solar panels, the grid and a battery bank. Actual operating priorities depend on the inverter model, configuration and approved grid connection.

01

Situation 1 — Daytime, grid available

During daylight, solar panels generate electricity that can supply household or business loads.

Depending on the system configuration, surplus solar energy can charge the battery. Additional eligible surplus electricity may be exported to the grid under the applicable net-metering or billing arrangement.

Solar Panels → Hybrid Inverter → Property Loads

Surplus Solar → Battery Charging / Permitted Grid Export

The battery can also be managed to retain a selected reserve for future power cuts.

02

Situation 2 — Daytime, grid power cut

A compatible hybrid inverter detects loss of grid supply and isolates the backup system from the utility network.

Subject to the system design and operating conditions, solar generation can continue supporting the designated backup circuits and battery.

Solar Panels + Battery → Hybrid Inverter → Essential Backup Loads

During an outage, the system must not feed electricity into the disconnected utility grid.

The available power depends on sunlight, inverter output, battery condition and connected loads. Some appliances may experience a brief interruption during changeover.

03

Situation 3 — Nighttime, grid power cut

At night, solar panels do not generate meaningful electricity. The hybrid inverter draws stored energy from the battery to supply the selected backup circuits.

Battery Bank → Hybrid Inverter → Essential Backup Loads

Backup duration depends primarily on usable stored energy and the actual electricity consumed by connected appliances.

Important: Hybrid backup is not unlimited power

A 5 kWh battery cannot supply every household appliance indefinitely. High-power equipment such as large air conditioners, heaters, cooking appliances and pumps can considerably increase the load.

This is why battery sizing and backup-circuit selection matter as much as the solar panel capacity.

04 · HOW MUCH BATTERY BACKUP DO YOU ACTUALLY NEED?

Start with your appliances—not the battery price

Before choosing a battery, identify what must continue working during an electricity outage.

For many households, priority loads include:

  • Ceiling fans and essential lighting
  • Wi-Fi router and phone charging
  • Refrigerator, subject to starting-current requirements
  • Television or computer
  • Selected other appliances within the inverter's supported capacity

Commercial properties may need backup for lighting, communications, security systems or specific equipment.

Understand kW and kWh

kW measures power. It helps describe how much electrical load the system can support at a particular moment.

kWh measures energy. It helps describe how much electricity a battery can store and deliver over time.

For example, a 5 kWh battery stores approximately five units of electrical energy at its stated nominal capacity. The amount available to appliances may be lower because of permitted depth of discharge, conversion losses and operating reserves.

How we recommend a battery size

Global Green Energy's system assessment should establish:

  1. Which appliances must operate during a power cut
  2. Their combined running load and starting requirements
  3. How many hours of backup are desired
  4. Whether daytime solar charging will be available
  5. Whether existing equipment can be safely integrated
  6. The space, ventilation and installation requirements

A bigger battery is not always the first answer. Prioritising essential circuits and selecting a suitable inverter can sometimes improve the practical value of a backup system without unnecessarily increasing storage capacity.

06 · WHAT EQUIPMENT MAKES A HYBRID SOLAR SYSTEM WORK?

WHAT EQUIPMENT MAKES A HYBRID SOLAR SYSTEM WORK?

Solar panels

The panels convert sunlight into DC electricity.

Panel capacity, roof orientation, shade, module characteristics and installation quality all influence generation.

Our featured Tata configuration uses nine 590–595 W DCR modules. Other supported configurations may use different capacities or approved equipment.

Hybrid inverter

The hybrid inverter is the central power-management equipment.

Depending on its model and design, it coordinates solar generation, battery charging and discharging, utility-grid interaction, monitoring and backup output.

Deye and Solis are among the inverter options available through our Tata and custom supply routes.

Important selection criteria include:

  • Compatible solar input and inverter output ratings
  • Battery type and communication compatibility
  • Supported backup loads and surge requirements
  • Single-phase or three-phase configuration
  • Grid connection and permitted operating modes
  • Monitoring and manufacturer support

Battery bank

The battery stores electrical energy for later use.

Its specification should be evaluated using nominal and usable energy, cycle-life conditions, supported discharge power, battery management, warranty terms and compatibility with the selected inverter.

A stated 15-year design life is not automatically a 15-year warranty. The written warranty certificate remains the authority for covered defects, exclusions and claims.

Mounting structure

A properly engineered mounting structure supports the panels and transfers loads safely to the roof.

Structure materials, corrosion protection, wind loading, roof condition and fastening arrangements should be assessed for the actual installation.

Electrical protection and backup circuits

Hybrid installations require suitable isolation, protection, earthing and wiring arrangements.

Important elements may include DC and AC protection, surge protection, appropriately rated cables, battery protection and properly configured essential-load circuits.

The exact protection arrangement is project-specific.

Why this matters: The cheapest-looking quotation is not necessarily the best complete installation. Customers should compare the full equipment list, protection scope and workmanship—not just panels and inverter capacity.

08 · HYBRID SOLAR VS STANDARD ON-GRID SOLAR

Which system should you choose?

Hybrid and on-grid system comparison
FeatureStandard on-grid solarHybrid solar with battery
Reduces grid electricity consumptionYesYes

Supplies power during a grid outageNormally noYes, to supported backup circuits when conditions permit

Initial equipment costGenerally lowerGenerally higher

Choose on-grid solar when…

Your main objective is reducing electricity purchases, grid supply is reasonably reliable and there is no significant need for stored backup energy.

Consider hybrid solar when…

Power cuts are a practical problem, you want to keep important loads running during outages, and you are willing to invest in suitable inverter and battery equipment.

Hybrid solar should be selected for the value of both generation and backup—not on the assumption that it will always generate greater financial savings than an on-grid alternative.

09 · HYBRID SOLAR PRICE, SUBSIDY & FINANCING

What determines the price of a hybrid solar system?

The final price depends on the complete configuration, including:

  • Number, model and capacity of solar panels
  • Hybrid inverter brand, model and output rating
  • Battery chemistry, capacity and manufacturer
  • Compatible control and monitoring equipment
  • Structure and roof installation requirements
  • AC/DC protection, cabling and earthing
  • Backup circuit arrangements and electrical modifications
  • Commissioning, documentation and after-sales scope

A 5 kWh battery configuration cannot be compared fairly with a much larger battery bank simply by looking at the solar panel capacity.

11 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can Tata Power Solaroof hybrid systems use Deye or Solis inverters?

Yes. Deye and Solis equipment is available within applicable Tata Power Solaroof kit/co-branded configurations as well as in independently supplied custom hybrid systems. Availability, compatible batteries and exact models must be confirmed for the proposed installation.

What solar capacity is included in the featured Tata hybrid system?

The featured configuration uses nine Tata panels rated at 590–595 W each, giving approximately 5.31–5.36 kWp of DC solar capacity.

The inverter's AC output rating is specified separately.

Is a 5 kWh battery enough for a home?

It depends on which appliances need backup and for how long. Essential lighting, fans and communication equipment may require much less power than pumps, air conditioners or heating appliances.

We recommend selecting backup circuits and calculating expected consumption before confirming the battery capacity.

Will hybrid solar work during a power cut?

A properly designed compatible hybrid system can power selected loads during grid outages, subject to sufficient available solar/battery energy, inverter capacity and the system's operating conditions.

Can a hybrid system operate without a battery?

Some hybrid-capable inverters support particular operating modes without a connected battery, but capabilities depend on the exact model. The battery-backed systems described on this page include storage because power-cut backup is a central customer requirement.

Can I run an air conditioner on hybrid solar?

Potentially, if the inverter, battery, wiring and backup circuits are designed for the AC's running and starting requirements. Operating an AC can significantly reduce battery backup duration.

How long does the featured battery last?

The proposed battery has been described with a 6,000-cycle rating and expected life of up to 15 years. The stated figures are subject to exact model verification against manufacturer documentation and test conditions.

Neither figure automatically represents a guaranteed service life or warranty period.

Can I get PM Surya Ghar subsidy on hybrid solar?

Eligibility depends on the installation's approved configuration and applicable programme requirements. An eligible residential rooftop solar installation may qualify for central subsidy, but battery storage itself does not create additional subsidy entitlement.

We confirm eligibility before quotation.

Can an existing home inverter or battery be reused?

Possibly, but not automatically. Compatibility, electrical safety, battery technology and the proposed system design must be checked before integrating existing equipment.

Is a Tata Power Solaroof hybrid system the same as a custom hybrid system using Tata panels?

No. Official Tata Power Solaroof equipment supply, project processing and warranty documentation must be distinguished from independently configured systems, even where some equipment brands overlap.
