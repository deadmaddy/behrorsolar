How the subsidy works

Under PM Surya Ghar, eligible residential consumers receive central CFA of ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for the additional capacity between 2 and 3 kW. The central CFA is capped at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above.

The additional ₹17,000 Rajasthan state benefit may apply to eligible beneficiaries under the solar-linked 150-units-per-month Nishulk Bijli scheme. Existing free-electricity enrollment alone does not confirm payment. Your connection’s state-scheme registration and DISCOM eligibility must be checked before this benefit is included in an estimate. State eligibility and disbursement are separate from central CFA redemption. ₹95,000 is the maximum combined assistance only where both central and state benefits apply.

We confirm eligibility before including the Rajasthan benefit in a customer's financial estimate.

Can a solar electricity bill reach ₹0?

A ₹0 monthly payable bill is possible with applicable Mukhya Mantri Muft Bijli Yojna benefits. A ₹0 annual payable bill is possible through export settlement with suitable system sizing. Actual results depend on solar generation, consumption and JVVNL import/export settlement; these outcomes are not guaranteed.

Rajasthan's separate free-electricity benefits, where applicable, can reduce the payable amount further. For some consumers, cash compensation and adjustments against fixed charges already paid can also affect the final payable amount.

Do you install solar for shops and industrial properties?

Yes. We assess commercial and industrial rooftops separately, using the property’s roof type and electricity-use pattern to size the system. The residential PM Surya Ghar subsidy does not apply to commercial and industrial connections.

Is PM Surya Ghar subsidy available for Kotputli Rural properties?

Yes, residential consumers in Kotputli Rural with a valid JVVNL domestic connection are eligible on the same terms as urban Kotputli applicants. Central CFA is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the additional capacity between 2 and 3 kW, capped at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above. Properties on agricultural or commercial tariffs need a separate eligibility check before applying.

Which discom handles net metering for Kotputli — and how long does approval take?

Kotputli falls under JVVNL (Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited). After installation, we submit the net metering application directly to JVVNL and coordinate the technical inspection on your behalf. Approval timelines vary with JVVNL's processing load, but we follow up directly so you don't have to chase the paperwork yourself.
