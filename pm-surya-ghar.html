SUBSIDY SLABS — 2026

PM Surya Ghar Rajasthan mein kitni subsidy milti hai?

Short answer: Central CFA is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or more. Rajasthan's 2026–27 Economic Review also records an additional ₹17,000 state subsidy for consumers covered by the state's applicable free-electricity / PM Surya Ghar category.

ELIGIBILITY

PM Surya Ghar ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?

Short answer: The scheme is for residential consumers with a grid-connected electricity connection and a suitable rooftop. The household should not already have taken another rooftop-solar subsidy for the same eligible installation. Rural households are also covered.

Typical eligibility
  • Residential electricity connection with the local DISCOM / JVVNL
  • A roof that can legally and safely host the proposed rooftop solar plant
  • Applicant / consumer details that can be validated on the National Portal
  • No duplicate rooftop-solar subsidy claim for the same eligible consumer / installation
  • Installation through a vendor registered for PM Surya Ghar
Not covered by residential CFA
  • Commercial or industrial electricity connections
  • Agricultural connections seeking residential PM Surya Ghar CFA
  • Installations that do not meet current DCR / equipment compliance requirements
  • Applications with unresolved consumer, bank or portal validation issues
  • Duplicate / previously subsidised rooftop-solar claims where the scheme does not permit another CFA

Important 2026 update: The Government has simplified approvals by waiving the technical-feasibility requirement and introducing auto load enhancement up to 10 kW under PM Surya Ghar. The portal and DISCOM still validate connection and interconnection details, so we confirm the current JVVNL workflow for each application before installation. Official process update ↗

DOCUMENTS & DETAILS

PM Surya Ghar apply karne ke liye kya documents chahiye?

Short answer: Keep your latest electricity bill / consumer number, identity details used by the portal, mobile number and bank details ready. Bank account details and a cancelled cheque are submitted for CFA redemption after commissioning. JVVNL or the portal may ask for additional documents depending on the connection or property.

01

Latest JVVNL Electricity Bill

Use the bill to enter the correct consumer / K-number, DISCOM and connection details. If the consumer name or number is wrong in JVVNL records, correct that first to avoid portal validation delays.

02

Applicant Identity & Contact Details

Keep the identity details requested by the National Portal, a working mobile number and email address ready. The portal's current fields should be treated as the final requirement because forms can change.

03

Roof / Ownership Authorisation, if Asked

If the applicant is not the owner or the DISCOM needs proof of authority to use the roof, keep ownership proof or written authorisation / NOC available. Exact JVVNL documentation can depend on the connection record.

04

Bank Details & Cancelled Cheque

After commissioning, bank details and a cancelled cheque are submitted through the National Portal for CFA redemption. Ensure the bank details entered on the portal are accurate.

Document requirements may be updated by MNRE, the National Portal or JVVNL. We check the live portal before submission rather than relying on an old static checklist.

Choose a Registered Vendor — रजिस्टर्ड वेंडर चुनें

04

Install DCR / ALMM-Compliant Solar Equipment

PM Surya Ghar requires eligible domestic-content solar modules and compliance with current MNRE rules. ALMM applies to approved solar PV modules (List-I) and solar PV cells (List-II, as applicable)—there is no "ALMM inverter list." Inverters must instead meet the applicable MNRE / BIS / scheme compliance and communication requirements.

We keep the DCR / module documentation and equipment compliance records required for commissioning.

Residential installation itself is usually completed quickly once the site and portal workflow are ready; exact timing depends on the project.

05

JVVNL Net Metering & Commissioning — नेट मीटर और कमीशनिंग

After installation, the plant details are submitted and the DISCOM completes the applicable net-metering / inspection / commissioning workflow. The commissioning certificate generated through the process is important for CFA redemption.

JVVNL timelines can vary with division workload, meter availability, site corrections and portal status, so we track each application rather than promising a fixed number of days.

06

Submit CFA Redemption & Receive Subsidy — सब्सिडी सीधे बैंक में

After commissioning, submit the required bank details / cancelled cheque and CFA redemption request on the National Portal. Government information states that the official process is designed for subsidy credit within 30 days; in March 2026 MNRE reported an average processing time of around 15 days after a correctly completed redemption request.

Rajasthan's additional ₹17,000 state subsidy is separate and applies only to consumers meeting the current state eligibility criteria; state disbursal timing can vary.

For PM Surya Ghar support / grievances, the national call centre number published by the Government is 15555.

JVVNL NET METERING

JVVNL net metering kya hota hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?

Short answer: A bidirectional meter records electricity imported from JVVNL and solar electricity exported to the grid. Your bill is then settled according to the current JVVNL / Rajasthan net-metering rules.

Daytime — दिन में

Your rooftop system first supplies the loads in your home. When generation is higher than immediate consumption, surplus energy can flow to the grid and is recorded as export by the bidirectional meter.

Night-time — रात में

Solar generation stops at night, so the home imports electricity from JVVNL. The bidirectional meter separately records this imported energy.

Your monthly bill — महीने का बिल

Import and export readings are settled under the applicable tariff and net-metering rules. Savings depend on system size, rooftop orientation, shading, weather, household consumption pattern and the current JVVNL billing framework—so a "zero bill" should never be guaranteed.

2026 process note: PM Surya Ghar has introduced auto load enhancement up to 10 kW and waived the earlier technical-feasibility requirement nationally. Connection-specific portal / DISCOM checks still apply.

Keep the commissioning certificate and portal reference details safely because they are important for CFA redemption and grievance tracking.

DCR, ALMM & INVERTER COMPLIANCE

DCR panels kyun zaroori hain? ALMM aur inverter compliance mein kya difference hai?

Short answer: PM Surya Ghar requires eligible domestic-content solar modules. MNRE's ALMM framework covers approved solar PV modules (List-I) and solar PV cells (List-II). Inverters are not "ALMM-listed"; they have separate MNRE / BIS / scheme compliance requirements.

DCR Requirement

DCR means the domestic-content requirements prescribed for subsidised rooftop-solar installations. The vendor should retain the required traceability / DCR documentation for the modules used in the project.

ALMM List-I & List-II

MNRE publishes ALMM List-I for solar PV modules and List-II for solar PV cells. Applicability can change with MNRE notifications, so the exact module / cell model should be verified against the current list before procurement.

Inverter Compliance

There is no ALMM list for solar inverters. Under PM Surya Ghar, inverters must meet the applicable safety, grid, communication / monitoring and scheme requirements issued by MNRE and relevant standards.

COMMON DELAYS & REWORK

PM Surya Ghar application delay ya reject kyun ho sakti hai?

Short answer: Most avoidable problems come from mismatched consumer details, incorrect portal data, non-compliant equipment, incomplete commissioning records, bank-detail errors or using a vendor / workflow that is not valid for the scheme.

01

Consumer details do not validate

Incorrect K-number, applicant name, mobile details or DISCOM selection can stop the application from progressing. Enter details exactly as shown in current JVVNL records and correct material discrepancies first.

02

Inverter does not meet scheme compliance

Do not look for an "ALMM inverter." Instead, verify that the inverter meets the current MNRE / BIS / grid and PM Surya Ghar communication requirements applicable on the installation date.

03

Module DCR / ALMM documentation problem

The selected module / cell configuration must meet the current domestic-content and ALMM requirements that apply to the project. Keep the vendor's supporting documentation available for commissioning.

04

Installation does not match portal / DISCOM workflow

Although the earlier technical-feasibility requirement has been waived up to the current scheme threshold, the application still needs to follow the National Portal and JVVNL interconnection workflow. Installing against incorrect portal data can create rework.

05

Bank or CFA redemption details are incorrect

Subsidy processing begins after commissioning and a valid CFA redemption request. Incorrect bank details, cancelled-cheque information or incomplete portal credentials can delay payment.

06

Vendor / commissioning records are incomplete

Use a vendor registered under PM Surya Ghar and ensure the installation details, net-metering record and commissioning certificate are correctly reflected on the portal before requesting CFA.

PM SURYA GHAR FAQ

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal — Behror & Neemrana

PM Surya Ghar subsidy seedha bank mein kab aati hai?

After JVVNL / DISCOM commissioning, the consumer submits bank details and the CFA redemption request on the National Portal. Government guidance provides for payment within 30 days, and MNRE reported in March 2026 that average CFA processing was around 15 days after a correctly completed redemption request. Actual cases can take longer if portal or bank details need correction.

Kya Neemrana Rural mein PM Surya Ghar milta hai?

Yes. The Government states that rural households with a grid-connected residential electricity connection can participate in PM Surya Ghar. The connection and installation must still meet the current National Portal and JVVNL requirements.

Kya subsidy pane ke liye pehle poora paisa dena padta hai?

In the standard direct-purchase flow, the system is paid for or financed and the CFA is credited after installation, commissioning and a successful redemption request. PM Surya Ghar also supports collateral-free loan options through participating public-sector banks; current rates and eligibility should be checked on the portal / bank at the time of application.

DCR panels aur ALMM inverter mein kya fark hai?

DCR relates to domestic-content requirements for eligible subsidised solar modules. MNRE's ALMM framework lists approved solar PV modules (List-I) and solar PV cells (List-II). There is no ALMM list for inverters; inverters have separate MNRE / BIS / PM Surya Ghar compliance requirements. See the DCR & ALMM section →

Kya commercial ya industrial property ke liye PM Surya Ghar milta hai?

No. The household CFA component of PM Surya Ghar is for residential electricity consumers. Commercial and industrial rooftop solar can still be attractive, but project sizing, tax treatment, tariff savings and ROI are evaluated separately from residential CFA.

Kya ab bhi JVVNL feasibility approval ka wait karna padta hai?

The Government has simplified PM Surya Ghar by waiving the technical-feasibility requirement and introducing auto load enhancement up to 10 kW. JVVNL still needs the application / connection and interconnection data to be correct, so we verify the live portal status before installation.

Agar subsidy delay ho toh kya kare?

First check that commissioning is complete and the CFA redemption request / bank details are correctly submitted on the National Portal. Use the portal grievance route if the status is stuck. The Government's PM Surya Ghar call centre number is 15555. Keep the application reference and commissioning certificate ready when raising a complaint.

Rajasthan ka extra ₹17,000 sabko milta hai?

No. Rajasthan's Economic Review 2026–27 records an additional ₹17,000 state subsidy for consumers covered by the relevant state scheme who apply through the PM Surya Ghar National Portal. Eligibility should be verified against the current state / DISCOM rules before treating ₹95,000 as the final subsidy amount.
