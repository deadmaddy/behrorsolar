SUBSIDY SLABS — 2026

PM Surya Ghar Rajasthan mein kitni subsidy milti hai?

For eligible residential installations in Rajasthan, central CFA is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for the next 1 kW. The maximum is ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or more. A larger system does not increase this central amount.

ELIGIBILITY

PM Surya Ghar ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?

PM Surya Ghar household CFA is for eligible residential consumers with a grid-connected electricity connection and a suitable rooftop. Rural households are also covered. The connection, installation and subsidy history must meet the current scheme conditions.

Typical eligibility
  • Residential electricity connection with the local DISCOM / JVVNL
  • A roof that can legally and safely host the proposed rooftop solar plant
  • Applicant / consumer details that can be validated on the National Portal
  • No duplicate rooftop-solar subsidy claim for the same eligible consumer / installation
  • Installation through a vendor registered for PM Surya Ghar
Not covered by residential CFA
  • Commercial or industrial electricity connections
  • Agricultural connections seeking residential PM Surya Ghar CFA
  • Installations that do not meet current DCR / equipment compliance requirements
  • Duplicate / previously subsidised rooftop-solar claims where the scheme does not permit another CFA

Issues to resolve: Consumer, bank or portal-validation errors must be corrected so the application can progress.

Select the DISCOM shown on your electricity bill. For a JVVNL connection, enter the consumer/K-number exactly as recorded by JVVNL.

DOCUMENTS & DETAILS BY STAGE

PM Surya Ghar apply karne ke liye kya documents chahiye?

Prepare your electricity connection and applicant details first. Keep installation records for commissioning, then submit bank details for CFA redemption.

01

At application — आवेदन के समय

Prepare your latest electricity bill, consumer/K-number, applicant details requested by the National Portal, working mobile number and email address. Keep roof ownership/authorisation or an NOC available if requested. Use the consumer details in the current DISCOM records and correct material discrepancies.

02

At installation and commissioning — स्थापना और कमीशनिंग के समय

Keep the installation invoice, exact module and inverter details, serial-number records, DCR traceability, photographs requested by the portal, meter/inspection records and commissioning certificate. Some records are uploaded by the vendor or DISCOM; this is a handover checklist, not a claim that the consumer must upload every item personally.

03

For central CFA redemption — केंद्रीय सब्सिडी के लिए

After portal commissioning, check your commissioning certificate and submit the bank account details, cancelled cheque and CFA redemption request required by the National Portal. Save the redemption reference and track the status. A lender may request banking documents earlier if you apply for finance.

The National Portal, lender or JVVNL may request additional documents for your connection or property. Follow the current requirements shown for your application.

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS — UPDATED 2026

Behror aur Neemrana mein PM Surya Ghar kaise apply kare?

Register, apply, choose a registered vendor, install compliant equipment, complete JVVNL commissioning, then request CFA redemption.

01

Register on the National Portal — पोर्टल पर रजिस्टर करें

Visit pmsuryaghar.gov.in ↗, select Rajasthan and the DISCOM shown on your electricity bill, and register using your consumer details and mobile number. For a JVVNL connection, select JVVNL. Save the application reference.

02

Submit the application and check connection details — आवेदन और डिस्कॉम जाँच

Submit the rooftop-solar application through the National Portal. For a complete domestic-category application up to and including 10 kW, a separate technical-feasibility study is not required. JVVNL carries out any commensurate sanctioned-load enhancement required under the rules. Application validation and the interconnection workflow still apply. Above 10 kW, technical feasibility is required.

Check the application and connection status before installation. The feasibility exemption does not remove metering, safety or commissioning requirements.

03

Choose a registered vendor — रजिस्टर्ड वेंडर चुनें

Select a vendor with valid PM Surya Ghar registration for the applicable area on the National Portal. Check the registered business name and obtain a written quotation with the exact equipment, installation scope, warranty and payment milestones. Tata channel-partner status and PM Surya Ghar vendor registration are separate checks.

04

Install compliant equipment

Use modules manufactured in India using Indian-manufactured cells, with verifiable DCR records, and equipment meeting the applicable ALMM, inverter and grid requirements. Check the exact models and retain the equipment and installation records for commissioning. See DCR, ALMM and inverter compliance →

05

Complete JVVNL net metering and commissioning — नेट मीटर और कमीशनिंग

After installation, submit the required plant records and request net metering through the applicable portal workflow. JVVNL completes the required meter, inspection and commissioning steps. Check that the commissioning certificate is available on the National Portal and retain a copy.

Track the application status and resolve any site, meter or document issues. An application, installation or feasibility exemption is not permission to energise the system before the applicable commissioning requirements are completed.

06

Request CFA redemption and track payment — सब्सिडी का अनुरोध और स्थिति

After portal commissioning, submit the required bank details, cancelled cheque and complete CFA redemption request. Save the reference and track the payment status. Incorrect bank details or incomplete commissioning/redemption records can delay processing. See the payment and delay FAQs →

JVVNL NET METERING

JVVNL net metering kya hota hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?

Short answer: A bidirectional meter records electricity imported from JVVNL and solar electricity exported to the grid. Your bill is then settled according to the current JVVNL / Rajasthan net-metering rules.

Daytime — दिन में

Your rooftop system first supplies the loads in your home. When generation is higher than immediate consumption, surplus energy can flow to the grid and is recorded as export by the bidirectional meter.

Night-time — रात में

Solar generation stops at night. A standard on-grid home then imports electricity from JVVNL, and the bidirectional meter records that import. Battery-backed systems operate according to their separate design.

Your monthly bill — महीने का बिल

Import and export readings are settled under the applicable JVVNL/Rajasthan rules. Solar can substantially reduce energy charges, but fixed charges, duties and other applicable amounts may remain unless waived under an applicable state benefit. A ₹0 final bill is not guaranteed. Your result depends on generation, consumption and the current settlement rules.

Standard on-grid solar shuts down during grid outages. Backup requires a separately designed system. Explore hybrid solar and backup options.

DCR, ALMM & INVERTER COMPLIANCE

DCR panels kyun zaroori hain? ALMM aur inverter compliance mein kya difference hai?

DCR, ALMM and inverter compliance are separate checks. PM Surya Ghar CFA requires eligible domestic-content modules. ALMM List-I covers solar modules and List-II covers solar cells. Inverters are not ALMM-listed; they have separate standards and scheme requirements.

DCR Requirement

For CFA, solar modules must be manufactured in India using Indian-manufactured cells. Ask for traceable DCR records for the actual modules installed. An Indian brand name or module assembly location alone does not establish compliance.

ALMM List-I & List-II

MNRE’s 4 August 2026 clarification exempts covered net-metering projects commissioned by 31 December 2026 from ALMM List-II for cells. Applicable List-I requirements for modules continue. This exemption does not relax PM Surya Ghar DCR. Check the project category, exact equipment and commissioning date against the current orders. MNRE clarification — 4 August 2026 ↗

Inverter Compliance

Check the exact inverter model’s applicable BIS/BEE, safety, grid and monitoring requirements. MNRE’s 17 August 2026 memorandum addresses secure communication, India-resident data and servers, OEM confirmation to REC, daily generation reporting and inclusion of the data logger in the inverter warranty. These obligations require equipment/OEM documentation; they are not an additional subsidy application for the homeowner. MNRE inverter memorandum — 17 August 2026 ↗

Before accepting a quotation

Ask for the exact module and inverter models, DCR traceability, applicable ALMM status, inverter/OEM scheme documentation, logger warranty and any connectivity charges in writing.

COMMON DELAYS & REWORK

PM Surya Ghar application delay ya reject kyun ho sakti hai?

Short answer: Most avoidable problems come from mismatched consumer details, incorrect portal data, non-compliant equipment, incomplete commissioning records, bank-detail errors or using a vendor / workflow that is not valid for the scheme.

01

Consumer details do not validate

Incorrect K-number, applicant name, mobile details or DISCOM selection can stop the application from progressing. Enter details exactly as shown in current JVVNL records and correct material discrepancies first.

02

Inverter does not meet scheme compliance

Check the selected inverter against the applicable scheme requirements. See the equipment compliance checklist →

03

Module DCR / ALMM documentation problem

The actual modules must have verifiable domestic-content records and meet the ALMM requirements applicable to the project and commissioning date. List-II exemptions do not waive DCR. Retain the supporting serial-number and equipment records.

04

Installation does not match portal / DISCOM workflow

Installing against incorrect portal or connection data can create rework. Follow the National Portal and JVVNL interconnection workflow.

05

Bank or CFA redemption details are incorrect

Subsidy processing begins after commissioning and a valid CFA redemption request. Incorrect bank details, cancelled-cheque information or incomplete portal credentials can delay payment.

06

Vendor / commissioning records are incomplete

Use a vendor registered under PM Surya Ghar and ensure the installation details, net-metering record and commissioning certificate are correctly reflected on the portal before requesting CFA.

PM SURYA GHAR FAQ

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal — Behror & Neemrana

PM Surya Ghar subsidy seedha bank mein kab aati hai?

After DISCOM commissioning is recorded on the National Portal, submit the required bank details and a complete CFA redemption request. Government guidance describes payment within 30 days; MNRE reported a national average of around 15 days after a correctly completed redemption request in March 2026. These are guidance and historical processing figures, not a guaranteed JVVNL payment date. Corrections or validation issues can take longer.

Kya Neemrana Rural mein PM Surya Ghar milta hai?

Yes. Rural households with a grid-connected residential electricity connection can participate, subject to the current scheme, installation and DISCOM requirements. Select the DISCOM shown on your electricity bill.

Kya subsidy pane ke liye pehle poora paisa dena padta hai?

In the standard direct-purchase route, payment or financing follows the written quotation. Central CFA is processed after installation, portal commissioning and a valid redemption request; it is not automatically deducted from the upfront price. Participating banks offer loan options subject to their current eligibility, terms and approval. A lender may request bank documents before installation.

DCR, ALMM aur inverter compliance mein kya fark hai?

DCR requires eligible Indian-manufactured modules using Indian-manufactured cells. ALMM List-I covers modules; List-II covers cells. Current List-II exemptions do not waive DCR. There is no ALMM list for inverters: they have separate applicable BIS/BEE, grid, monitoring and PM Surya Ghar requirements. See the equipment-compliance section for the current dated guidance.

Kya commercial ya industrial connection ke liye household CFA milta hai?

No. The household CFA component is for eligible residential electricity consumers. A commercial or industrial rooftop project needs its own sizing and financial assessment without deducting residential CFA.

Kya ab bhi JVVNL feasibility approval ka wait karna padta hai?

For a complete domestic-category application up to and including 10 kW, a separate technical-feasibility study is not required. JVVNL carries out any commensurate sanctioned-load enhancement required under the rules. Application validation, safety, metering and commissioning requirements continue. Above 10 kW, technical feasibility is required. This threshold is separate from the ₹78,000 central-CFA cap.

Agar subsidy delay ho toh kya kare?

Check that portal commissioning is complete and that the CFA redemption request and bank details have been correctly submitted. Save the application reference, commissioning certificate and redemption reference. If the status remains unresolved, use the National Portal grievance route or the PM Surya Ghar call centre on 15555. Check any conditional Rajasthan state payment separately with the relevant DISCOM/state-scheme channel.

Rajasthan ka extra ₹17,000 sabko milta hai?

No. It may apply to eligible beneficiaries under Rajasthan’s solar-linked 150-units-per-month Nishulk Bijli scheme. Existing free-electricity enrollment alone does not confirm payment. Check the connection’s state-scheme registration and DISCOM eligibility before counting it. Up to ₹95,000 means maximum central CFA of ₹78,000 plus the conditional ₹17,000 state benefit.
