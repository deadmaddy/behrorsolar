ABOUT BEHROR SOLAR

Who you're actually trusting with your roof.

Behror Solar is Global Green Energy's local business for Behror and Neemrana, led by GGE partner Mahendra Yadav. You get a team close to home, backed by an authorised Tata Power Solaroof channel partner with an established record across Rajasthan.

Why we exist

It started with a bad rooftop system — my own.

My first installation, from a local outfit, came with under-performing panels, a flimsy mounting structure and no proper earthing. Sorting it out sent me deep into panels, structures, inverters and vendor contracts — and showed me how many homeowners across Behror and Neemrana were making expensive decisions without reliable local guidance.

For my second system I approached Global Green Energy, an authorised Tata Power Solaroof channel partner whose founder I already knew. We shared the same view: this region deserves properly engineered solar and honest guidance, not simply the lowest quote. I invested in GGE and took responsibility for Behror–Neemrana, bringing the local, hands-on service I wish I had received the first time.

That's still why Behror Solar exists: to help people here make informed decisions, and to build systems that last decades, not seasons.

— Mahendra, Behror–Neemrana Lead

What makes us different

Premium engineering, not the lowest quote

  • Structures built for 30 years, not two. Hot-dip galvanised mounting chosen for a service life measured in decades, not seasons.
  • We test what we sell. We ran our own TOPCon vs Mono PERC panels side by side on the same roof and measured the difference — because we'd rather show you real numbers than repeat a brochure.
  • Honest, brand-agnostic advice. We fit both Tata (for brand assurance) and Premier TOPCon (for value and performance), and tell you plainly which panel suits your roof and budget.
