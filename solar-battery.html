COST

How much does a solar battery cost in Behror & Neemrana?

Battery cost is custom-sized to your home, because it depends on how much backup you need and which capacity fits your load. Standard 12.8 V, 25.6 V and 51.2 V units are available off-the-shelf; larger stacked systems are quoted per project.

Unlike a fixed-size panel system, a battery is priced around your backup goal — a few essential circuits through the evening costs far less than running the whole house, including the AC, all night. Rather than publish a misleading number, we size the battery to your actual load during a free survey and give you one honest, all-in figure. One thing to know up front: the PM Surya Ghar subsidy covers your solar panels, not the battery — so the battery is a separate, optional investment in backup and independence.

SIZING

What size battery do I need for my home?

Start with what must stay on during a cut. A small 12.8 V–51.2 V unit covers essentials; a 5–10 kWh system runs most of a home comfortably overnight; and villas, farmhouses or whole-home-with-AC backup step up to larger stacked systems.

Voltra's range is modular, so the battery grows with your needs — from a compact single unit up to around 40 kWh for homes (and 60–80 kWh stacked for villas and farms), with 1 to 16 modules connected in parallel. You don't over-buy; we start from your real load and add only what your outages require.

CHEMISTRY

Which battery is best — LiFePO₄ or lead-acid?

For solar backup, LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) is the clear choice. It lasts 4–6× longer, uses far more of its stored energy, needs no maintenance, and handles Rajasthan's heat better than a traditional lead-acid (tubular) battery.

LIFESPAN

How long does a solar battery last?

A quality LiFePO₄ battery is rated for 6,000–8,000+ charge cycles with a design life of up to 15 years. At one cycle a day that is well over a decade of daily use — several times the life of a lead-acid battery.

LiFePO₄ also degrades slowly and isn't harmed by partial charging, so it holds most of its capacity for years rather than fading fast. Every Voltra battery has a multi-level Battery Management System (BMS) that monitors each cell, balances charge and prevents over-discharge — which is what protects that long rated life in real-world use.

RAJASTHAN HEAT

Is a solar battery safe in Rajasthan's summer heat?

Yes. LiFePO₄ is the safest lithium chemistry — it does not go into thermal runaway the way other lithium types can. The batteries we install are rated to discharge up to 50°C and carry IP54/IP65 protection and a multi-level BMS.

It's a fair question in a region that crosses 45°C in summer. LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) is chemically stable under heat, and each unit's Battery Management System watches cell temperature and shuts things down safely long before anything becomes a risk. Wall-mounted units are sealed to IP65 against dust and weather — well suited to Behror and Neemrana rooftops and utility areas.

RETROFIT

Can I add a battery to my existing solar system?

Often yes — but it usually needs a hybrid inverter to manage the battery, panels and grid together. If your current system is a standard on-grid setup, we assess whether to add a hybrid inverter alongside the battery during the survey.

A battery only delivers power-cut backup when paired with an inverter that can island from the grid. If you're planning solar and backup together, our hybrid solar system combines the panels, hybrid inverter and battery as one package. Not sure which route fits? SOLAR BATTERY — FAQ

Common questions about solar batteries in Behror & Neemrana

Does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy cover the battery?

No. The PM Surya Ghar subsidy applies only to the solar panel capacity — up to ₹78,000 for a 3 kW system. The battery and hybrid inverter are a separate investment in backup and are not covered by the central scheme. See our PM Surya Ghar subsidy guide for what is covered.

Can the battery be expanded later?

Yes. Voltra's home batteries are modular — you can start with the capacity you need now and add modules in parallel later (up to 16), so the system grows with your household without replacing what you already have.

Does a solar battery need maintenance?

No routine maintenance. Unlike lead-acid batteries, LiFePO₄ needs no water top-up or special ventilation. The built-in BMS manages charging and cell health automatically. It simply works in the background.

Will a battery run my air conditioner during a power cut?

It can, but AC is a heavy load that drains a battery quickly — a 1.5-ton AC can pull ~1,800 W. A larger battery is needed to run AC for meaningful hours. We size the system around whether AC backup is a priority for you.

Do I need a battery if I already have on-grid solar?

Only if you want power during outages. Standard on-grid solar shuts off during a power cut by law. A battery with a hybrid inverter is what keeps your home running when the grid is down — that is its whole purpose.
