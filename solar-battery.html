BESS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

What is a home BESS?

BESS means Battery Energy Storage System. In a home, it is the battery, BMS and controls that store electricity and make it available when you need it.

For power-cut backup, the battery works with a compatible hybrid inverter. During normal operation, solar can run the home and charge the battery. During an outage, the inverter isolates the backed-up circuits from the grid and supplies them from solar and/or the battery.

COST

How much does a solar battery cost in Behror & Neemrana?

Battery cost is custom-sized to your home because the useful buying numbers are energy capacity (kWh), power requirement (kW), battery chemistry and inverter compatibility. Nominal voltage — 12.8 V, 25.6 V or 51.2 V — is a system specification, not a measure of backup time.

Unlike a fixed-size panel system, a battery is priced around your backup goal — a few essential circuits through the evening costs far less than running the whole house, including the AC, all night. Rather than publish a misleading number, we size the battery to your actual load during a free survey and give you one honest, all-in figure. Battery storage can be included in a qualifying PM Surya Ghar rooftop-solar system, but central CFA is calculated from eligible solar-module capacity. Adding battery capacity does not increase the central subsidy amount.

kW VS kWh

Two numbers matter: how much you can run, and for how long

Battery quotations are easier to compare when power and energy are kept separate.

kWh

Energy capacity

This is the size of the energy tank. More usable kWh generally means longer backup at the same load.

Answers: “How long will it run?”
kW

Power capability

This is how much load the inverter and battery can supply at one time. Starting surges and heavy appliances matter here.

Answers: “Can it run this load?”

A large kWh battery does not automatically mean every appliance can run simultaneously. We check inverter continuous power, surge requirement and battery discharge limits together.

RECHARGE TIME

How quickly will the battery recharge after a power cut?

Recharge time depends on battery capacity, available solar power, the inverter's charging limit and how much electricity the home is using while the battery is charging.

A 10 kWh battery cannot be given one honest recharge-time number without knowing the charging power available to it. During design, we check the PV array, inverter charge rate and whether grid charging is enabled, then estimate recharge time for your actual configuration.

CHEMISTRY

Which battery is best — LiFePO₄ or lead-acid?

For the solar backup systems we design, LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) is our preferred chemistry because it offers high usable capacity, long cycle life, no water top-ups and better thermal stability than traditional lead-acid batteries.

Shopping in “Ah”? Here's the translation to kWh

Batteries are still often sold as “150Ah” or “200Ah”. Nominal energy is calculated as volts × amp-hours ÷ 1,000: a 12 V 200Ah battery therefore holds about 2.4 kWh on paper. Actual usable energy is lower and depends on the battery's permitted depth of discharge, condition and system losses. When comparing quotes, compare usable kWh, rated power and warranty terms—not amp-hours alone.

LIFESPAN

How long does a solar battery last?

There is no single cycle-life or lifespan figure for every LiFePO₄ battery. The rating depends on the exact model and test conditions, while real service life also depends on temperature, depth of discharge, charge rate and operating pattern.

We state the selected battery's warranted performance and cycle-life conditions from its current manufacturer documentation in the quote. A battery management system monitors and protects the pack, but installation conditions and correct system sizing still matter.

RAJASTHAN HEAT

Is a solar battery safe in Rajasthan's summer heat?

LiFePO₄ is widely chosen for stationary storage because of its comparatively strong thermal stability. The units we select have model-specific operating-temperature and enclosure ratings, plus a BMS that monitors cell conditions and protects against abnormal operation.

It's a fair question in a region that crosses 45°C in summer. We do not treat an IP rating as permission to put a battery anywhere: model limits, shade, direct sun, ventilation, cable routing, clearances and manufacturer installation requirements all matter. We prefer an appropriate indoor or protected outdoor location wherever the selected product allows it.

A battery only delivers power-cut backup when paired with an inverter that can island from the grid. SOLAR BATTERY — FAQ

Common questions about solar batteries in Behror & Neemrana

Does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy cover the battery?

Battery storage may be included in a qualifying PM Surya Ghar rooftop-solar installation, but central CFA is calculated from eligible solar-module capacity—not battery capacity. Under the current central structure, eligible residential systems receive ₹30,000 per kWp for the first 2 kWp and ₹18,000 for the next 1 kWp, with no additional central CFA beyond 3 kWp; adding battery capacity does not increase that amount. Eligibility and approval remain subject to the scheme, portal and DISCOM requirements. See our PM Surya Ghar subsidy guide.

Can the battery be expanded later?

Often yes, but only within the selected battery family's permitted expansion rules and with compatible modules, firmware, inverter capacity and protection. We confirm the allowed module count and whether later expansion is supported before installation.

Does a solar battery need maintenance?

LiFePO₄ batteries need no water top-up or lead-acid-style routine battery maintenance. We still recommend periodic inspection of electrical connections, protection devices, inverter/BMS logs and the installation environment.

Will a battery run my air conditioner during a power cut?

It can, but AC is a heavy load that drains a battery quickly — a 1.5-ton AC can pull ~1,800 W. A larger battery is needed to run AC for meaningful hours. We size the system around whether AC backup is a priority for you.

Do I need a battery if I already have on-grid solar?

Only if you want power during outages. Standard on-grid solar shuts off during a power cut by law. A battery with a hybrid inverter is what keeps your home running when the grid is down — that is its whole purpose.

What is the difference between kW and kWh in a battery system?

kWh is the amount of usable energy stored and mainly affects backup duration. kW is power — how much load the battery and inverter can supply at one time. Both must be sized correctly.

How long does a LiFePO₄ battery take to recharge?

There is no single recharge time. It depends on battery capacity, available solar power, inverter charging limits, household consumption while charging and whether grid charging is enabled. We estimate it from the selected system design.
